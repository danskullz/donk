# Accessibility review of the comps (Phase 0)

*2026-11-01. Target: WCAG 2.2 AA (SCOPE.md §5.7). This is the pass that can be done on static comps: measured contrast, target sizes, text sizes and markup structure, with fixes applied. It is not a screen-reader test and not a test on real devices; those are Phase 2 (end of this file).*

Run it again with `python -I a11y_check.py` (output in `a11y_results.txt`). It needs only headless Edge, which is already on this machine.

## What was measured

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| **Text contrast**, 17 text and background pairs from `tokens.css` | All pass 4.5:1 after one fix (below). Lowest: `error` on the wrong-answer row, 4.8:1. |
| **Non-text contrast**, 7 pairs (outlines, focus ring, icons) | All pass 3:1. Lowest: control outline on a card, 3.2:1. |
| **Target size at 360 × 640** | Every control is at least 44 px tall on Home and on all question screens. On the Map screens the tabs are 44 × 40, list rows 42 and the secondary buttons 40; all are well above the WCAG 2.2 AA floor of 24 px. |
| **Target size at 1280 × 720** (mouse) | 32–38 px. Fine for a pointer; the 24 px AA floor is met everywhere. |
| **Smallest text at 360 × 640** | 11 px (hints, captions, chips, the map legend). Body text is 12–13 px, headings 16 px and up. |
| **Structure**, 15 pages | `lang` set, `main` landmark on every page, every image has `alt`, every button has a name, every `role="img"` SVG has a label, Home has an `h1`, progress bars have `role="progressbar"`. CSS has `prefers-reduced-motion`, `forced-colors`, `:focus-visible` and a screen-reader-only class. |

## Problems found and fixed in the comps

1. **Muted text was 4.49:1 on the hover surface** (the suggestion list shows zone names in muted text on it). `sub` went from `#93969a` to `#9a9da1`: 6.2:1 on the page, 4.9:1 on the hover surface.
2. **The selected-region ring failed 3:1 on a "known" region** (lime ring, olive fill: 2.0:1). The ring now has a dark band on both sides, so it is judged against the page background (11.4:1), not against whichever region it touches.
3. **Tabs of one letter were 8 px wide** (T, A, B). Tabs now have a minimum width: 44 px on phones, 40 px elsewhere.
4. **Suggestion rows, the footer buttons, the zoom buttons and the Home buttons were 36–40 px on a phone.** They are 44 px now.
5. **The 10 px map legend on phones** is 11 px.
6. **Progress bars had `aria-label` on a plain `div`**, which assistive technology ignores. They are `role="progressbar"` with values.
7. **A reveal was silent.** The session and pin-it reveals now carry a polite live region ("Wrong. It is Connector. You chose Window."). In the app, focus also moves to the Continue button.
8. **Home had no heading.** It has a screen-reader-only `h1`.
9. **Windows high-contrast mode** (`forced-colors`): correct and wrong rows, the selected tab and row, the highlighted suggestion and the primary button get real outlines and borders, because background colours are discarded in that mode.

## Accepted exceptions, with reasons

- **The two lowest mastery fills are dim on purpose** (New 1.2:1, Seen 2.4:1 against the page): an unstarted map should look unstarted. The information is not carried by colour alone: Seen has hatching, Learning has dots, and every region's state is also written out in the list on the Map screen and in the accessible name of its button. Familiar, Known and Automatic differ in lightness (3.2, 5.7 and 11.4:1), which survives colour-vision differences.
- **Touch targets on the Map screen are 40–44 px tall at 360 × 640**, not 44. Making them all 44 would take roughly 20 px more from a map that is already about 224 px tall. The map regions themselves are the primary target and get a tap-to-zoom assist (SCOPE.md §5.4).
- **11 px text** exists only for secondary information (key hints, captions, legend). Nothing the learner must read to answer is below 13 px.

## Requirements for the Phase 1 build (what the comps cannot show)

| Part | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Whole app | Tab order follows the DOM: bar, main, footer. `Esc` leaves a session. `?` opens the key list, `/` focuses find. One `h1` per screen (visible or screen-reader-only). |
| Home map picker | A grid with a roving tabindex: arrows move between cards, `Enter` opens one. Each card's name reads like "Mirage, 41 percent, 3 due". |
| Multiple choice | `role="group"` named "Answers"; the options are buttons; keys `1`–`4`. Answering does not move focus until the reveal, then focus goes to Continue and the live region announces the result. |
| Typed answer | A real `<input role="combobox">` with `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` and `aria-activedescendant`, and a listbox of at most five options. `↑` `↓` pick, `Tab` completes, `Enter` answers. The comps' blinking caret is a stand-in; the real one is the browser's. |
| Pin it | The map is a labelled group; each region is a button with a roving tabindex and a name such as "Connector, familiar, due". Arrow keys move to the nearest region in that direction, `Enter` pins. A visible control switches the question to multiple choice (SCOPE.md §4.10). |
| Map pan and zoom | Buttons `+`, `−` and "fit"; keys `+`, `−`, `0`; `Shift`+arrows pan. Dragging is never the only way to pan (WCAG 2.5.7): selecting a region or a list row recentres the view, and the minimap can be tapped. |
| Tabs, pager, switch | `tablist`/`tab`/`tabpanel` with `aria-controls` and arrow-key movement. Pager buttons are named "previous page" and "next page", and "page 2 of 2" is announced. Hard mode is `role="switch"` with `aria-checked`. |
| Speed call | The only timed exercise. Settings can lengthen the flash time or turn the mode off (WCAG 2.2.1). |
| Alt text | A screenshot's alt text is generated from the callout's blurb and the camera's facing, never "screenshot". |
| Motion | Every transition is gated by `prefers-reduced-motion`, with a Settings override (SCOPE.md §5.8). Nothing flashes. |
| Zoom and small windows | Below 1024 × 600, or at text zoom beyond about 200 %, the shell switches to a scrolling layout (SCOPE.md §5.3 rule 8). The comps do not implement this fallback yet; it is a Phase 1 item with a Playwright test at 200 % and 400 %. |

## Phase 2 review (cannot be done on comps)

- A screen-reader pass (NVDA with Firefox and Chrome, VoiceOver on a phone) through one full session, the map screen and Settings.
- Keyboard-only run of every flow, including the Editor-free parts of Hard mode.
- 200 % and 400 % zoom, and Windows high-contrast themes, on the built app.
- A colour-vision simulation of the mastery ramp on the real map art.
- A check on a real phone with the soft keyboard open (the comps are tested at 360 × 340 as a stand-in).
